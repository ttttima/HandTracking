      Sensitivity

      Distances are measured against the size of your palm, so they hold at any distance from the camera. Changes apply straight away and are saved.

      Saved settings

      Every setting and keybind is saved to a file as you change it, and loaded when the app starts.

      Keybinds

      Choose what each gesture does while Control my PC is on. Either hand can trigger a keybind. "When held" waits until the gesture has been held for about a second. Pinch is the mouse: a quick pinch clicks, pinch and hold drags.
